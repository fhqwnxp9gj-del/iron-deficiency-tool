Diagnosis of iron deficiency
Patient populationLab value
Non-pregnant adultsFerritin <30 mcg/L
Adults with concomitant inflammationFerritin >30 mcg/L AND transferrin sat <20–30%
Pregnant adultsFerritin <50 mcg/L
Pediatric patients (<18y)Ferritin <20 mcg/L
Pediatric (<18y) + inflammationFerritin >20 mcg/L AND transferrin sat <20%

Side effects/AEs:
Dark stools, upset stomach, bloating, diarrhea, nausea, and constipation.
Management of side effects:
  • Increase consumption of fiber, fluids, add PEG
  • Take the iron pill every other day
  • Try a different formulation

Treat iron deficiency
Use oral iron formulations above; escalate to IV iron if needed
Transfusion if needed: hemodynamic instability, or generally Hb <50–60 g/L in otherwise healthy and chronically anemic patients
